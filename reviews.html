Review detail

Inception · Sci-Fi / Thriller · 2010
★★★★★

A heist film folded inside a dream. Nolan spends the first act teaching you the rules and the rest of the film breaking them, and somehow the emotional core — a man trying to get home to his kids — never gets lost under all the machinery. The zero-gravity hallway fight is still one of the best practical set pieces of the decade, and Zimmer's score does half the heavy lifting. It rewards a second watch.

— @minh
